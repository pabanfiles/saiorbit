## Method 2: iOS Live Photo Conversion (iPhone Only)

If you are an iPhone user without internet access to an external tool, you can use iOS's native Live Photo workaround:

1. Open TikTok on your iPhone.
2. Tap the **Share** button on the video.
3. Scroll through the bottom options and select **Live Photo**.
4. Open the **Apple Photos** app and locate the newly saved Live Photo.
5. Tap the three dots icon in the top right corner.
6. Select **Save as Video**.

### Limitations of Method 2:
- A small static watermark remains visible in the corner.
- Audio fidelity is often compressed.
- Video resolution is slightly downgraded compared to the raw CDN source.

## Method 3: Desktop Developer Tools / Network Inspection

For technical desktop users, you can inspect the browser network panel to capture the raw video stream:

1. Open TikTok in Google Chrome or Mozilla Firefox on your desktop.
2. Open Developer Tools (`F12` or `Ctrl + Shift + I`).
3. Switch to the **Network** tab and filter by **Media**.
4. Play the TikTok video; look for the streaming request ending in `.mp4`.
5. Right-click the request and choose **Open in new tab**, then press `Ctrl + S` to save.
