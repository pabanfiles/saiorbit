Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2025 • Effective Immediately

At Orbit Video Downloader (a Sai Orbit product), the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by Orbit Video and how we utilize it in accordance with the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the Indian Information Technology Act (2000), and Indian Digital Personal Data Protection Act (2023).

1. Consent

By using our website and digital tools, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

2. Information We Collect

Orbit Video is designed as a client-friendly utility. We do NOT require you to register an account, log in with social media credentials, or submit sensitive personally identifiable information (PII) to use our video and audio conversion features.

  • Log Files: Like almost all web servers, Orbit Video utilizes standard log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.
  • Contact Form Data: If you contact us directly via our Contact page, we receive your name, email address, the contents of the message, and any attachments you may send. This information is solely used to respond to your inquiry and is never sold or traded.
  • Video URLs: URLs you submit for processing are used only for the duration of the download and are never stored on our servers beyond the immediate session.

3. Cookies & Tracking

Orbit Video does not set tracking cookies for advertising or analytics. Any cookies you may receive are from third-party services that may be integrated in the future (see Section 4 below). You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

4. Third-Party Services

Some features may rely on third-party services. These may include:

  • Cloudflare — for CDN, DDoS protection, and content delivery (privacy policy: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/)
  • Render.com — for backend API hosting (privacy policy: https://render.com/privacy)
  • Google Fonts — for typography (privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy)

These services may collect connection data such as your IP address as part of normal traffic handling. Orbit Video has no control over their data practices.

5. GDPR & DPDP Data Protection Rights

We want to make sure you are fully aware of all of your data protection rights. Every user is entitled to the following:

  • The right to access – You have the right to request copies of your personal data.
  • The right to rectification – You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate.
  • The right to erasure – You have the right to request that we erase your personal data, under certain conditions.
  • The right to restrict processing – You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal data.
  • The right to object to processing – You have the right to object to our processing of your personal data.

6. India Digital Personal Data Protection Act Compliance

In accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP), 2023 of India, Orbit Video processes digital data fairly and transparently. We do not store or process personal biometric, financial, or sensitive information on our servers.

7. Children's Information (COPPA)

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. We encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, and/or monitor and guide their online activity. Orbit Video does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13.

8. Data Retention

Submitted video URLs and downloaded media are processed in-memory or in temporary storage and are automatically deleted within 5 minutes of delivery. We do not maintain a permanent library of processed content.

9. Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact our Data Compliance team at privacy@saiorbit.com or via our Contact Form.
