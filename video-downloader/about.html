About Orbit Video

Engineering the Future of Media Archiving

Fast, accessible, and privacy-conscious media tools for creators worldwide.

Our Story

Orbit Video Downloader was born out of frustration with existing web downloaders. For years, the internet has been overrun by tools bogged down with deceptive popup ads, intrusive redirects, forced extension installations, and sluggish download speeds.

We set out to engineer an alternative: a clean, software-as-a-service grade web platform designed with modern glassmorphism aesthetics, lightning-fast streaming pipelines, and deep respect for user privacy and device safety.

Orbit Video is part of the Sai Orbit family of tools — a growing collection of everyday web utilities built with the same philosophy: simple, fast, and free.

Our Mission & Vision

🎯 Our Mission

To provide free, high-speed, and secure access to public digital video and audio backups without deceptive monetization, watermarks, or device bloatware.

🔭 Our Vision

To be the most trusted media utility in South Asia and globally, championing open-source software, digital privacy, and fair use.

Why Users Choose Orbit Video

  • Zero Ads & Malware: Clean interface devoid of aggressive fake download buttons or intrusive redirects.
  • Direct Stream Speed: Integrated chunk streaming begins your file download in less than 1 second.
  • Studio Quality MP3 Conversion: Built with backend FFmpeg audio processors that deliver authentic 320kbps acoustics.
  • Universal Device Support: Engineered mobile-first to work smoothly across iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac, and Windows.
  • Privacy First: We do not store your URLs or downloaded files. All media is wiped within minutes after delivery.

Technology Stack

Orbit Video is maintained by a small agile team passionate about open-source technologies: yt-dlp, Python, FastAPI, FFmpeg, and modern web technologies. We continuously audit our backend servers to ensure high uptime, compliance with global privacy regulations (GDPR, India DPDP), and safety standards.
