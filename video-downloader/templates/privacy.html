Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2025 • Effective Immediately

At Sai Digital (accessible from our website domain), the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by Sai Digital and how we utilize it in accordance with the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the Indian Information Technology Act (2000), and Google AdSense publisher policies.

1. Consent

By using our website and digital tools, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

2. Information We Collect

Sai Digital is designed as a client-friendly utility. We do NOT require you to register an account, log in with social media credentials, or submit sensitive personally identifiable information (PII) to use our video and audio conversion features.

  • Log Files: Like almost all web servers, Sai Digital utilizes standard log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.
  • Contact Form Data: If you contact us directly via our Contact page, we receive your name, email address, the contents of the message, and any attachments you may send. This information is solely used to respond to your inquiry and is never sold or traded.

3. Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookies

Google is one of our third-party vendors on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet.

Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. Third-Party Advertising Partners

Some of the advertisers on our site may use cookies and web beacons. Our advertising partners include:

  • Google AdSense

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective advertisements and links that appear on Sai Digital, which are sent directly to users' browsers. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. These technologies are used to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and/or to personalize the advertising content that you see on websites that you visit.

Note that Sai Digital has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers.

5. CCPA & GDPR Data Protection Rights

We want to make sure you are fully aware of all of your data protection rights. Every user is entitled to the following:

  • The right to access – You have the right to request copies of your personal data.
  • The right to rectification – You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate.
  • The right to erasure – You have the right to request that we erase your personal data, under certain conditions.
  • The right to restrict processing – You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal data.
  • The right to object to processing – You have the right to object to our processing of your personal data.

6. India Digital Personal Data Protection Act Compliance

In accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP), 2023 of India, Sai Digital processes digital data fairly and transparently. We do not store or process personal biometric, financial, or sensitive information on our servers.

7. Children's Information (COPPA)

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. We encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, and/or monitor and guide their online activity. Sai Digital does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13.

8. Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact our Data Compliance team at privacy@saidigital.tools or via our Contact Form.
